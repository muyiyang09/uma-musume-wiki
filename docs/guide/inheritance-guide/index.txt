3:T24f3,<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold mt-10 mb-4 pb-2 border-b border-border text-white">什么是因子</h2>

<p class="mb-4 text-gray-300">每次育成结束后，系统会根据该次育成的结果生成<strong class="text-white font-semibold">因子（Factor）</strong>。将带因子的赛马娘设为<strong class="text-white font-semibold">种马（父母）</strong>，新育成时父母的部分因子会被继承到下一代，长期积累可大幅提升育成上限。</p>

<hr class="my-8 border-border"/>

<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold mt-10 mb-4 pb-2 border-b border-border text-white">四种因子详解</h2>

<h3 class="text-xl font-semibold mt-8 mb-3 text-primary-light">蓝色因子（属性因子）</h3>

<tr><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">属性</td><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">获得条件</td><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">星数上限</td></tr>

<tr><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">速度因子</td><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">最终速度 ≥ 600</td><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">★★★</td></tr>
<tr><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">耐力因子</td><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">最终耐力 ≥ 600</td><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">★★★</td></tr>
<tr><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">力量因子</td><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">最终力量 ≥ 600</td><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">★★★</td></tr>
<tr><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">根性因子</td><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">最终根性 ≥ 600</td><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">★★★</td></tr>
<tr><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">智力因子</td><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">最终智力 ≥ 600</td><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">★★★</td></tr>

<tr><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">属性值</td><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">星级</td></tr>

<tr><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">600~799</td><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">★</td></tr>

<blockquote class="border-l-4 border-primary/50 pl-4 py-2 my-4 bg-bg-alt rounded-r-lg text-dim italic">💡 <strong class="text-white font-semibold">刷蓝因子目标</strong>：1100+ 单属性出 ★★★，双属性 1100+ 出双 ★★★ 是优质种马标配。</blockquote>

<h3 class="text-xl font-semibold mt-8 mb-3 text-primary-light">红色因子（适性因子）</h3>

<p class="mb-4 text-gray-300">育成中获胜过的 <strong class="text-white font-semibold">G1 赛事</strong> 会生成对应的适性因子。继承后提升对应适性等级（最高 S）。</p>

<tr><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">适性</td><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">说明</td></tr>

<tr><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">芝 / ダート</td><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">场地适性</td></tr>
<tr><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">短 / 英里 / 中 / 长</td><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">距离适性</td></tr>
<tr><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">逃 / 先 / 差 / 追</td><td class="p-3 border-b border-border text-sm ">跑法适性</td></tr>

<strong class="text-white font-semibold">重点</strong>：适性从 A → S 的收益巨大，适性 S 时属性加成更高、技能发动率提升。

<h3 class="text-xl font-semibold mt-8 mb-3 text-primary-light">绿色因子（固有技能等级因子）</h3>

<p class="mb-4 text-gray-300">育成结束时固有技能达到 Lv.6 即可获得。继承后可提升新马娘的固有技能初始等级。</p>

<h3 class="text-xl font-semibold mt-8 mb-3 text-primary-light">白色因子（技能因子）</h3>

<p class="mb-4 text-gray-300">育成中获取的技能有小概率生成对应的白色因子。继承后育成开始时直接获得该技能。</p>
